GAMAY 佳美
  • High acid
  • Low-medium tannin
  • Red fruit: raspberry, red cherry, red plum
  • Typically light body
  • Typically unoaked
  • Some winemaking aromas (Carbonic Maceration): banana and candy
    • Meant for early consumption
  • France: Beaujolais AOC, Beaujolais Village AOC, Fleurie AOC (Cru Beaujolais)
  • map
  • WHAT IS CARBONIC MACERATION: intra-cellular fermentation, no yeast, oxygen is displaced by Carbon Dioxide and whole clusters are placed in a closed tank. Grape sugars are triggered by enzymes to start fermenting… only about 4% alcohol, then the tank is opened and fermentation is finished with yeast addtion.
